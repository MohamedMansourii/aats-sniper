> mission's completion is *earned*, not asserted. This gamifies the swarm honestly: **points are awarded ONLY
> when the Acceptance Gate (`MISSION-BOARD.md §ACCEPTANCE GATE`) is fully satisfied** — dual-G3 PASS + suite

## The prime goal
Take AATS to **elite, competitor-parity, all de-risk-only**, then produce an **honest GO/NO-GO edge verdict on
recorded real data**. Real capital only after GO + CEO authorization. "Elite" ≠ "profitable-by-decree" — it
means best-in-class *capability* with honest measurement.

## The two hard blockers that no amount of engineering removes
1. **Corpus size (Milestone C/E).** The regime model (M2-CP-03/06) and the edge proof (Milestone E) need
   **≥ 3,000 recorded real launches**. The live PumpPortal feed is accruing them now; do NOT train or "prove"
   on synthetic data — that is self-deception and the audit will fail it. Build the architecture now; train
   and prove when the data exists.
2. **The verdict can be NO-GO.** Milestone E can honestly come back NO-GO. That is a *successful* outcome of
   the process (it protected the capital), not a failure to hide. Report it straight to the CEO.

## How to run the reward ledger (keep it current every wave)
When a mission's Acceptance Gate is satisfied and committed:
1. Mark it ✅ in `MISSION-BOARD.md` and `ELITE-ENHANCEMENT-ROADMAP.md`.
2. Add its points to the milestone tally in the table above.
3. If a milestone hits 100%, flip its Status to ✅ and announce the 🏅/🏆 reward in the CEO status report.
4. Update `STATE.md §3 DONE LEDGER` with the commit hash.
Keep the ledger honest: **partial/unreviewed work earns 0** until the gate passes (E16 is the live example —
its points are withheld until M-E16-REVIEW passes).

## Definition of "elite" (the acceptance bar for the whole program)
AATS is "elite" when: (a) it detects genuine launches across all four venues on real data; (b) it fuses
KOL-call + smart-money + adversarial-sentiment as de-risk selectivity; (c) it has the full exit arsenal
(TP-ladder, trailing, ratchet, timeout, insider-dump, delayed-honeypot, LP-unlock) proven; (d) it has a
calibrated regime/exit model that either beats the existing exit engine net-of-cost **or honestly stays
silent**; (e) it has passed the edge proof with a recorded verdict. Elite is measured, never declared.
