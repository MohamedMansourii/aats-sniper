2 · The Reality Check (read before spending a cent)

Three widely-sold myths cause the most wasted money in this space. Clearing them up protects your budget.

Myth 1 — "Microsecond" trading

False — and a red flag. Solana produces a block every ~400 milliseconds. The fastest, most expensive bots on earth react in sub-40ms, and only with servers co-located next to validators. A strong retail setup reacts in ~50–300ms. Microsecond trading does not exist on Solana for anyone. Any product promising "microseconds" is misleading you. The real game is milliseconds, and being faster than rival bots — not magic.

Myth 2 — "The AI already has a live feed"

The bot accesses live Solana data — through infrastructure you provision. This was already proven: on a free Helius key, the bot decoded real pump.fun mainnet launches, point-in-time correct. It is real — just rate-limited on the free tier. Upgrading the feed (this report) turns "real but slow" into "real and competitive."

Myth 3 — "You need Phantom"

You do not need Phantom — at all. Phantom is a human browser wallet. An automated bot signs with its own dedicated keypair inside an isolated signer (by design: your key never lives in Phantom or in the bot's memory). Do not buy, connect, or worry about Phantom.

10 · ROI Framework — How to Decide Every Future Purchase

Before any spend, ask these four questions in order. If the answer to any is "no", don't buy yet.

  1. Is this unlocked by a milestone I've already proven? (e.g., don't buy execution infra before edge proof.)
  2. Does it move a metric that decides GO/NO-GO, or does it only help after GO? Edge-proof spend (Tier 0) is the highest ROI because it produces the decision. Post-GO spend only matters if there's a GO.
  3. Is it the cheapest thing that fully answers the question? A free feed that proves the edge beats a $3k feed that proves the same edge.
  4. Does it reduce risk or only chase speed? Custody, caps, and the safety stack are always worth it. Raw speed is only worth it once an edge exists to exploit.

11 · Glossary (plain English)

RPCThe "phone line" your bot uses to talk to the Solana blockchain (read data, send trades).
Geyser / Yellowstone gRPCA high-speed "firehose" stream of everything happening on-chain, in real time. The bot is built for this.
LaserStreamHelius's managed version of that firehose, with a "no missed data" guarantee.
ShredStreamAn even-earlier view of transactions (50–100ms sooner), via Jito.
Jito / bundles / tipsA system to submit trades with priority and MEV protection; you pay a small "tip" in SOL per trade.
SWQoS (stake-weighted QoS)Priority access to validators based on stake — how pro bots get their trades in first.
EdgeA real, repeatable money-making advantage — after all costs (fees, tips, slippage). The thing we must prove.
Edge proof / walk-forwardA rigorous historical test that the strategy beats a simple baseline without "cheating" by seeing the future (no lookahead).
Point-in-time / no lookaheadOnly using information that was actually available at the moment of decision. Critical for an honest test.
CorpusThe recorded dataset of launches the edge proof runs on.
DRY_RUNThe hard safety switch: the bot simulates everything and touches no real money until you explicitly authorize it.
Custody / signer / VaultHow the trading key is stored and used safely, isolated from the rest of the bot.
Co-locationRenting a server physically near the validators to shave milliseconds. Expensive; only worth it once an edge exists.
